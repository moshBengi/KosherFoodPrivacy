Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Ingredient Scanner application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects user information.

Information We Collect

The App may access the device camera to allow users to scan food ingredient labels.

The App processes text extracted from images of product labels. This text may be temporarily sent to a secure server to provide ingredient analysis functionality.

The App does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or location.

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App, which includes scanning and analyzing food ingredient lists.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Data Storage

Scanned ingredient history may be stored locally on the user's device to allow users to review previous scans.

No personal data is permanently stored on external servers.

Camera Permission

The App requests permission to use the device camera solely for scanning food ingredient labels. The camera is not used for recording video, taking photos without user interaction, or collecting biometric data.

Data Security

Communication between the App and the server is performed over secure HTTPS connections. Reasonable measures are taken to protect data during transmission.

User Control

Users may stop using the App at any time. Users may delete the App to remove locally stored data.
